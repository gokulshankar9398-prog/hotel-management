Frequently Asked Questions

Standard check-in begins at 3:00 PM, and check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Early arrival and late check-out can be requested upon booking and are subject to availability. Our lounge and spa are always at your disposal if your room is being prepared.
We offer full flexibility on direct bookings. You can cancel or modify your reservation up to 48 hours prior to your scheduled arrival date without any penalty fees or lock-ins.
All Penthouse and Private Villa reservations include complimentary private airport transfers via luxury sedan or speedboat. For other suites, VIP private chauffeur transfers can be selected in the booking add-ons at checkout.
We welcome families warmly and offer dedicated kids' nature clubs, nanny services, and family pool areas. Small companion pets (under 25 lbs) are welcomed in select garden villa suites with luxury pet amenities.
